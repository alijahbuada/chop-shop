How to play

  • 1–0 jump to that pad's cue and play. Tapping a pad works too.
  • Shift + number sets that pad to the current spot. An empty pad also sets itself when hit.
  • [ and ] nudge the last pad 0.05s earlier or later. Hold Alt for 0.01s.
  • Space plays or pauses. Backspace clears the last pad.
  • Cues save in this browser for each video, so you can come back to a chop.
